**What to Look for When Hiring a Story Mapping Facilitator**

When vetting a consultant for your startup, look for the following criteria:

* **Startup DNA:** Avoid traditional enterprise consultants who rely on months-long discovery phases. Fast-growing startups need someone who can run a high-impact, 1-to-2-day workshop and deliver a prioritized backlog immediately.
